About ABC Collision

Brooklyn’s Trusted Body Shop for Everyday Drivers to High-End Exotics

At ABC Collision, located at 1279 Utica Ave in Brooklyn, we believe auto body repair should be straightforward, flawless, and stress-free. Managed by Steve, Howie, and Ali, our family-run repair center treats every customer like family.

Whether you drive a daily commuter Honda, an everyday SUV, or a luxury exotic like a Lamborghini Urus, Mercedes G-Wagon, or Rolls-Royce, we deliver factory OEM restoration standards. We use computerized frame measuring, PPG & Glasurit paint blending, and thorough quality inspections before you take the keys.
